DeJournal Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Applies to the DeJournal Android app and website

The short version

DeJournal is a private journal and daily planner. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't have servers that receive your journal, we don't use analytics or advertising, and we don't sell or share your data.

What DeJournal stores, and where

Your tasks, reminders, notes, ratings, reflections, highlights, photos, expenses, study sessions, places, screen-time records and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read this storage. The developer never receives any of it.

Photos you pick from your gallery are copied into DeJournal's private storage. DeJournal doesn't read the rest of your gallery.

Network requests

  • Maps: when you open the Places map, map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Like any website, those servers can see your IP address and which map area is being shown. You can turn map tiles off in Settings → Privacy. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • AI summaries (website only, off by default): if you turn this on and consent, only a short summary of the chosen period is sent to the AI server you configured. That summary includes counts, ratings, task and highlight titles, and totals. Photos, locations and note text are never sent. AI isn't available in the Android app.

Backups

On Android, your DeJournal data may be included in your device's own Google backup, if you have Android backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, and the developer can't access it. You can also export a backup file yourself from Settings → Data → Export.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries at any time, delete all location history from the Places page, or erase everything from Settings → Data → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Children

DeJournal isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
